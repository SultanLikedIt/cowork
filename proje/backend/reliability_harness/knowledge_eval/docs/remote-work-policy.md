# Remote Work Policy

Version 3.2, effective 1 January 2026. Owner: People Operations.

# Eligibility

Employees who have completed their six-month probation period may apply for remote work. Roles that require on-site equipment, such as laboratory technicians and warehouse staff, are not eligible.

# Working Days

Eligible employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Tuesdays and Thursdays are anchor days on which every team member is expected in the office.

# Equipment

The company provides a laptop, a second monitor and a headset. Employees may claim up to 4,000 TL once every two years for an ergonomic chair or desk, with a receipt.

# Internet Allowance

Remote employees receive a monthly internet allowance of 750 TL, paid with the salary. The allowance is not paid for months in which the employee worked fully on-site.

# Working From Abroad

Working from another country is allowed for at most 20 working days per calendar year and requires written approval from both the manager and the Legal team at least two weeks in advance.

# Security Requirements

Company data may only be accessed over the corporate VPN. Public Wi-Fi networks in cafes or airports must not be used without the VPN. Laptops must be locked whenever unattended.

# Termination of Remote Work

The manager may end a remote work arrangement with fourteen days' written notice if performance targets are not met for two consecutive quarters.
